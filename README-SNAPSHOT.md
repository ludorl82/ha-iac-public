# Smart home — public summary

What the Home Assistant in [Ludo's lab](https://labodeludo.dev/) is made of:
its integrations, how many entities of each kind, how its automations are
triggered, and its voice chain — generated from the private configuration
repository, which is not published.

Deliberately absent: the names of people, rooms, devices, entities and
automations; any time, schedule or routine; how many people or phones there
are. The configuration of a home describes the people in it, and renaming
things does not hide what the house does or when.
